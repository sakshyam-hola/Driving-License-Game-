🏍 Nepal Driving Licence Trial

Commit 8 of 10 - The 5 km per hour Figure 8 speed limit is removed. Speed in Figure 8 is now only gear top speed, throttle, brake and engine braking. Going fast can still fail through the outer line and island rules, not through speed enforcement.

Category A - Bike. A simplified version of the Department of Transport Management trial, on one flat test ground. Finish all stations to get your score.

Route (in order)

  • 1. Figure 8 - official layout: two 3 m islands, centres 4.5 m apart on the centre axis, 1.5 m lane around each. Enter from the RIGHT side and follow the top-bar hint, step by step: turn south at entry, go east along the south side, north through the middle, clockwise around the right island (north / east / south), north through the middle again, counter-clockwise around the left island (north / west / south), then exit east on the right (south) lane. Entering from the left, or riding in reverse, is an instant Wrong Entry Direction fail (-100 pts). There is NO artificial speed limit in the Figure 8 area any more (removed 2026-10-29): speed there is entirely yours - manual gear, throttle, and engine braking. Gear 1 at low speed is still the way through the tight turns; going fast widens your line and can still put you over the outer line or into an island, and those penalties still apply. The Figure 8 track surface is completely clean: one single merged 8-shaped surface with one continuous white boundary line, islands 0.1 m high with cones - no arrows, floor markers or guide overlays there at all, whatever the Arrows toggle says. Use the top-bar hint and, in Rider POV mode, the mini-map.
  • 2. STOP line - come to a full stop for 0.5 s at the white STOP line, just before the plank.
  • 3. Narrow plank - 10 m long, 50 cm wide, slightly raised. Keep both wheels on it, and do not stop on it (that counts as a foot down).
  • 4. U-Turn station - north-side pocket, just after the plank: turn left off the main lane at x = 10, into a semicircle lane 2 m wide (outer span 10 m, outer radius 5 m, inner radius 3 m, centre (6, -4)). First press L (PC) or IND (mobile) for the left signal - entering the arc without it is -5. Then hold LEFT through the north semicircle (east -> north -> west), staying in the lane: a first touch outside the white borders is -10, staying outside for 2 s while moving fails the trial, and going the wrong way (around the south side) fails. Holding LEFT works here even though full lock alone would cut inside: inside the arc only, steering is capped to the exact partial lock for the 4.0 m lane centre, so a held LEFT follows the lane. Exit heading south at x = 2, then left again to east on the main lane.
  • 5. Traffic light signal - eastbound, after the U-turn: stop line at x = 16. The light is red on arrival and turns green after a random 2-4 s. Stop fully before the white line while it is red; crossing the line on red is -5. If you arrive after it has already turned green, go.
  • 6. Road bumps - 5 speed bumps, eastbound, centres x = 20, 22, 24, 26, 28, each 1 m long, 2 m wide, 10 cm high. Cross them in Gear 1, under 12 km/h, straight on: the wheels roll over each bump one at a time (the 1.34 m wheelbase is longer than a 1 m bump), the chassis rises up to about 5 cm with a small suspension bounce, and each bump slows you slightly. Crossing faster than 12 km/h, in a gear other than 1, or at a steep entry angle (over 30 degrees) costs -5 once, and crossing faster than 12 km/h or at over 20 degrees makes the handlebar wobble for about 1 s. Skipping bumps (reaching the finish with fewer than 5 crossed) is -5 at the finish.
  • 7. Inclined / declined ramp stops (final station) - eastbound on the main lane (z = 0), after the bumps: ride up the 6 m, 10° ramp, stop fully for 0.5 s on the uphill (white line + STOP sign halfway up, at x = 35), restart smoothly in Gear 1 without rolling back more than 0.5 m, ride over the 3 m flat top at 1.0 m height, then stop fully for 0.5 s on the downhill (second white line + STOP sign halfway down, at x = 44, holding the brake - coasting alone cannot hold the bike on the decline), then down the 6 m decline and east through the finish gate at x = 50.

Scoring (implemented stations only)

Figure 8 = 20, Narrow plank = 10, U-Turn = 10, Traffic light = 10, Road bumps = 10, Ramp stops = 10. Total 70 points, pass mark 70% (49 points). The real DoTM test is 100 points / 7 categories; this game remains a simplified implemented subset, not a full 100-point rescale - no per-station 100-point values were given, so none are invented. First island touch: -5. Second island touch, staying outside the Figure 8 outer line for 2 seconds while moving (a brief overshoot is only a warning - steer back inside; the timer pauses when you are stopped, so stopping on the line to think does not fail you), Wrong Entry Direction (left entry or reverse riding, -100 pts), or leaving the plank: fail. Skipping the STOP line: -5. Touching a U-turn border: -10 once, staying outside it for 2 s while moving: fail, going the wrong way in the U-turn: fail, entering the U-turn without the left signal: -5. Skipping the U-turn entirely (reaching the finish without it): U-turn points lost, and the light, bumps and ramp after it do not count either, so skipping it cannot pass. Skipping either ramp stop (uphill or downhill), or rolling back more than 0.5 m after a ramp stop: -5 each (ramp score floored at 0, so missing both stops cannot take the ramp below 0). Running the red light: -5. Crossing a bump too fast (over 12 km/h), in a gear other than 1, or too steeply (over 30 degrees): -5 once. Skipping bumps: -5 at the finish. Bumps score is floored at 0, like the ramp.

Controls

  • Gears (manual, no reverse): N (gas does nothing, bike coasts), Gear 1 - max 10 km/h, quick pickup, best for Figure 8 and the plank, Gear 2 - max 18 km/h, Gear 3 - max 25 km/h, for straights. The trial starts in Gear 1, and every Retry restarts in Gear 1. The current gear is shown in the top bar as Gear: [N] / [1] / [2] / [3]. Shifting down to a lower gear while fast engine-brakes you down to that gear's top speed; shifting to Neutral coasts with no engine braking.
  • PC: Arrow keys or WASD to steer / accelerate / brake. Space = brake. Shift up: Shift, PageUp, or R (while playing). Shift down: Control, PageDown, Z, or F. One gear per press - holding the key does not run through gears. L = left signal on / off (for the U-turn; the top bar shows Signal: LEFT / OFF and an amber light blinks on the bike). The trial now starts in Rider POV, with the scooter cockpit (handlebar, grips, levers, mirrors, backlit speedo) at the bottom of the screen. C = camera (chase / top-down / Rider POV, in that order). In Rider POV, Q / E look left / right up to 60 degrees. On the fail / finish screen, R still means Retry, as before.
  • Mobile: on-screen ◀ ▶ GO / STOP buttons (multi-touch works - hold GO and steer at the same time), GEAR + / GEAR - next to GO / STOP (tap while holding GO and steering - it shifts instantly and does not cancel them), CAM cycles the camera, IND toggles the left signal for the U-turn, and in Rider POV mode LOOK buttons appear at the bottom centre.
  • Physics (grounded): the bike tracks the front wheel - sideways velocity is damped out in ~0.2 s, so it does not slide sideways. Releasing gas in Gear 1 engine-brakes hard (10 km/h to a stop in ~0.8 s), and braking below 3 m/s is sharper (12 m/s²) and snaps to exactly zero below 0.5 m/s, for a precise stop at the STOP line. Steering in Gear 1 is sharpest; minimum turn radius is 1.8 m and steering does nothing at a standstill. Inside the U-turn arc only, steering is capped to the 4.0 m lane centre (see the U-turn item above), so holding LEFT there is a clean 180 deg turn, not a cut inside.
  • Guide arrows: Arrows: ON / OFF (top bar, and the button below) shows or hides directional arrows and yellow route dashes outside the Figure 8 zone. Figure 8 itself always stays clean, with or without arrows.

The road bumps are now a scored station on the main route (see above), not a decoration. Category B (Car) is still not scored or playable - bike only.
